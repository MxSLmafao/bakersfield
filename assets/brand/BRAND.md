# Bakersfield brand sheet

## Logo

- Mark: pixel croissant with a dark brown French mustache.
- Source: `assets/brand/logo-mark-source.jpg` (generated with Gemini 3.1 Flash Image via Higgsfield, status: approved).
- Style: soft 16-bit pixel art. Soft pixel edges and gentle gradients. Not harsh 8-bit.

## Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Cream | `#F6E7CA` |
| Highlight | Butter | `#FAD488` |
| Croissant | Honey | `#F9B35F` |
| Croissant | Amber | `#EA9346` |
| Croissant | Bake | `#D06C2E` |
| Accent | Crust | `#A8572D` |
| Ink | Mustache | `#462217` |

Colours were taken from the logo pixels, not chosen by eye.

## Typography

- Headings and display: **Pixelify Sans** (Google Fonts, SIL OFL). Large sizes only. Use it for the wordmark, section titles and big numbers.
- Body and UI: **DM Sans** (Google Fonts, SIL OFL). Use it for all running text, buttons and labels.
- Rule: body text is the constraint. Pixel face is for display only.

## Voice

- Warm, plain and proud. Short sentences. No invented facts (menu, prices, hours and location come from the client).
